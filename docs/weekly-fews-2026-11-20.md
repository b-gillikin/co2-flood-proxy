# Weekly FEWS literature intake — 20 November 2026

**Supporting source.** González-Planet, Klotz and Juez (2026), *Learning from
dense meteorological networks: a regional LSTM model for streamflow simulation*.
EGUsphere preprint under review for HESS.
[Publisher record](https://doi.org/10.5194/egusphere-2026-5583).

The authors compared a regional station-based LSTM with an earlier
catchment-average benchmark in the Ebro. They report better daily streamflow
simulation, including lower errors at higher flows, on a chronological holdout.
Unseen catchments were left for future work. The comparison changes the input
representation and the way the model processes it. Cite as supporting context
when discussing the limits of averaged weather inputs. Our AlphaEarth vintage
contrast keeps the weather inputs fixed, so this study supplies no attribution
for that result.

document `cbc74ee1-1d1e-32da-b485-1b1290fb4d61` in both chapter collections.
