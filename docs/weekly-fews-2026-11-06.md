## Obeng2026Forecast

[From forecast to alert: an open-data AI flood early warning system with reservoir monitoring for the White Volta Basin](https://nhess.copernicus.org/articles/26/5667/2026/) — published; DOI 10.5194/nhess-26-5667-2026.

**Use:** Strong keeper: open-data FEWS and critical evaluation.

Read critically: no one-day benefit over persistence; full-feature linear regression is competitive and marginally stronger at high flow at 3/5 days. The independent 2004–2007 test contains no Orange/Red observations; extreme-category results rely on leave-one-year-out evaluation. The GloFAS comparator is reanalysis, not as-issued forecasts. Version provenance is internally unclear (direct v4 versus indirect older-v2.1 wording). Monthly reservoir proxies and retrospective availability do not establish operational input availability. A dissemination architecture does not demonstrate routine deployment. No validation of our models or general local-over-global superiority follows.

Mendeley document: `b4c2fad8-72f1-344b-afc8-4f35c5eefefc`; PDF attached.

## Aadhar2026Flood

[Role of hydrometeorological variables and catchment area to flood generation over the monsoonal climate region of India](https://hess.copernicus.org/articles/30/6747/2026/) — published; DOI 10.5194/hess-30-6747-2026.

**Use:** Background: rainfall heterogeneity and antecedent conditions.

Useful hydrological interpretation of spatial rainfall, antecedent state and catchment scale. Godavari/VIC monsoonal results, including longer accumulation windows, do not establish transferable Limburg timescales or causal effects. No additional analysis or variables are required by retaining this paper.

Mendeley document: `64130e87-5c39-3975-b23c-f724ee3d8e8b`; PDF attached.

## Shen2026Alteration

[Network-based subbasin-scale mapping of streamflow alteration in Ontario, Canada](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/18/7915/2026/) — published; DOI 10.5194/essd-18-7915-2026.

**Use:** Background: network context for regulation.

Network-based potential regulation is distinct from measured alteration magnitude and timing. It does not prove agency labels incorrect, establish low-head-weir effects, validate structure detection or estimate economic damage.
