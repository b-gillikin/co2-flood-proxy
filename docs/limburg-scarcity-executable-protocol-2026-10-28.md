## Question, population and clock

Can satellite AI help assess developing high water when ground-monitoring data are limited? Retain all 481 shared rainfall episodes, all eight target gauges, and all four issue offsets (0/6/12/24h). Removing an observing gauge does not remove its verification outcomes or its cases. Source gaps remain source gaps. Rebuild/read the original next-24h outcomes independently of masks; first future hour is issue+1h. Report every unscored opportunity and reason. Do not sample easier storms.

The common assessment clock is the original observed-prefix regional 10mm landmark. Ground-rainfall scarcity changes the assessment inputs, not that reference clock: the estimand is performance at common scheduled situations, not whether a degraded system would recognize a trigger. No forecast-issuance, inundation or warning-effectiveness claim follows.

## Exact scenario matrix

Use separate axes, with no full Cartesian product:

- Rich basin reference: available 72h hourly discharge histories at all eight tributary gauges and Sint Pieter/Venlo, local rain and season. Rich local-only and three Maas dropout controls (none, Sint Pieter only, Venlo only) identify what richer context contributes.
- Controlled local density: 3/6/12/24h intervals anchored to a current reading, keeping latest-observation age unchanged where the source has that reading. Separate fixed UTC reporting schedules at the same intervals, exhaustively averaging all 3/6/12/24 phases. The latter estimates density plus the resulting observation age. The 1h level aliases the rich reference.
- Local intermittent loss: 25/50/75% extra masking, scattered or two-state clustered with mean missing runs 6/12/24h; **100 shared continuous-timeline realizations** per fraction/pattern. Source values do not determine masks. Fractions share uniform innovations, giving nested severities within a pattern. Realizations persist across successive issue times. Zero loss aliases rich data; 100% local-channel loss is one explicit endpoint, not repeated under four equivalent pattern names.
- Spatial coverage: all subsets retaining 6, 4, 2 or 0 of the eight tributary observing gauges, with the all-eight reference retained. This is **128 subsets including all/none**, not a random station sample. Mainstem context remains a named separate component. Report target selected/unselected and which physical/country roles remain, as well as the aggregate network-size result. Include an all-discharge-absent endpoint removing both tributary and Maas inputs; local discharge absence alone still permits peer rescue.
- Historical scarcity: cap earlier donor/calibration histories at 3, 6 or 9 years. This is a cap, not a requirement that excludes early cases. All archives remain strictly earlier than each query; report their actual lengths and donor counts.
- Rainfall scarcity: 3/6/12/24h reporting-block coarsening, all UTC phases, plus absence of the rainfall feature block. Use only blocks completed by issue; average within completed blocks and causally hold the last completed rate over the unfinished current tail. Record actual rainfall-report age. Never use a future block endpoint to reconstruct the current tail. Seasonal date information remains known. This is temporal information degradation, not a simulation of deleting stations underlying gauge-adjusted radar.
- Combined reduced monitoring: (a) four retained tributary feeds, each reporting six-hourly, with no Maas feeds; exhaustively average all 70 four-gauge subsets × six UTC phases; (b) four retained tributary feeds with 50% clustered gaps of mean 12h, no Maas feeds, using 100 reproducible joint network/mask realizations. For (b), networks are uniformly drawn from the same 70 subsets, independently of outcomes. These are paired information scenarios, not event samples. Every target sees the same combined-profile network/timeline in a replication. The single-axis temporal scenarios remain local-feed loss with other monitoring intact. Do not call those single-axis conditions a simultaneous regional outage.

Only the specified input channels disappear. Historical verified donor response labels and target verification remain held. Report nominal masks, newly hidden originally observed values, effective total gaps, observation age and remaining station counts. A 0% condition need not be a perfectly complete source record. Claimed frequency is not silently replaced with a loss rate.

## Responsive features and capable conventional comparators

Replace the earlier three-reading signature **for this version only** with the ordered, actually observed 72h hourly discharge history at each channel. Each available coordinate is a known time relative to issue. This makes ordinary thinning affect the information actually used. It does not introduce new training or a new hydrological model.

Primary conventional treatment compares the retained query coordinates against the corresponding coordinates of earlier finalized historical donor histories. Unobserved query coordinates contribute no fictitious zero and no value-based distance. For real gaps in historical donor inputs, causally hold within that donor's history, then use the earlier-archive coordinate mean where no prior within-window observation exists. Those donor values were available in the earlier finalized archive; hidden current-query values never set preprocessing. Historical caps restrict this calibration too.

This models contemporary scarcity at historically monitored sites, not never-gauged basins or a removal of all historical Q knowledge. Audit selected donor measured, forward-held and mean-filled coordinate fractions and clean-source support. A declared sensitivity repeats the rich reference, local-Q absence and both combined profiles with a shared conventional/EO donor pool requiring at least 80% genuinely measured Q coordinates in each query-active channel and finite rain descriptors used by the query. This 80% level is our diagnostic choice, not a literature standard or a chapter stopping rule. Report the resulting archive restriction/fallback; adequate support does not guarantee perfect measurements. The primary analysis retains source uncertainties, with clean-verification and clean-selected-donor diagnostics.

A supporting conventional treatment forward-holds only retained query observations. No backward fill of an unknown beginning; no post-issue endpoint. Last-known local-Q persistence and a generic earlier verified historical distribution remain simple fallbacks/comparators. A masked value is never recoded as a genuine discharge zero.

For each discharge channel normalize squared coordinate distances by the sum of the earlier-archive expected squared pair differences at the retained coordinates. Use only earlier closed episodes, independent of query outcomes. Constant/unidentified coordinates contribute no ranking information; report support. Rainfall totals (6/24/72/168h), preceding-24h maximum and sine/cosine season use earlier-archive feature standardization. Combine four available groups equally: rain/season, local history, mean peer-channel history distance, mean Maas-channel history distance. Within peer/Maas groups use equal available channels. Thus seven peers do not automatically receive seven times the weight of the local channel. This is contextual matching, not a claim that downstream Maas conditions cause upstream responses.

Donors are same-gauge prior regional storms. Their 24h response must be complete before issue; episode end plus the inherited 12h closure interval must also precede issue. Exclude the query's entire storm. Five distinct donor storms are primary, ten supporting; retain smaller ensembles and record actual sizes. No p99/current hidden Q/future peak anchors or area-transfer assumptions enter native-unit predictions. Pool errors in mm/h using held catchment areas, while retaining per-gauge native units.

## EO comparison, coverage and interpretation

Use all ten held frozen EO representations, four completed weekly frames primarily and the completed-month snapshot as declared sensitivity. Reuse exact pre-landmark contexts, fixed through the issue sequence; no new extraction. Unit-normalize vectors, use squared Euclidean distance (equivalent ranking to cosine), then normalize by expected earlier available-vector pair distance. This deliberate simpler distance differs from the earlier coordinate-IQR metric; all new rich/scarce comparisons use this same specification. Old numerical estimates are preserved, not pooled as if the algorithms were identical.

Fuse equal squared-distance weights, 0.5 conventional and 0.5 EO. No tuning to scored outcomes. For each model preserve broad conventional, exact EO-supported conventional, and EO augmentation on that same donor subset. Unsupported EO falls back to broad conventional, then applicable persistence/history if needed. Separate representation increment, archive restriction and total effect. Keep source quality, membership, event/country, EO support and actual mask replication visible. NL/DE comparisons describe these sites; both German targets are on the Wurm and are not independent country samples.

TTM and AlphaEarth remain supporting representations, not falsely relabelled as run in every masked condition. Cached complete-history TTM vectors contain withheld information and cannot be reused here. Any subsequent TTM comparison needs permitted-history inference and a declared gap treatment. Annual AlphaEarth remains a landscape/transfer control, not current-state truth. Soil/snow remains a later physical-context control; none of these requires holding up this primary EO comparison.

## Scores, uncertainty and artifacts

Score the actual empirical ensemble, not a hidden fitted forecast head: mean hourly CRPS over the next 24h, +6/+12/+24h CRPS, mean-trajectory RMSE, peak magnitude/timing, inverse-CDF 80% coverage and width. Flat series have undefined peak timing, not invented zero-hour success. Smaller ensembles retain their actual count. These empirical intervals are not assumed calibrated confidence intervals.

Repeated masks/phases/network subsets do not create independent storms. Store mask-averaged case estimates, their variability and valid/EO/full-member frequencies. Keep sufficient per-replication, per-storm paired CRPS numerators/denominators for **999 joint mask × whole-storm/year bootstrap draws**, shared across methods. Source-cohort gauge/issue weights are fixed before comparing methods. For deterministic scenarios, the replication draw is trivial. Report block counts and nonfinite draws without silently discarding them. K5 CRPS has inferential priority; K10 and secondary endpoints are declared diagnostics. Compare rich/scarce errors on paired cases and keep unavailable predictions/verification in coverage ledgers.

Exact estimand and resampling: report issue offsets separately, giving each represented target site equal mass and each valid paired case-mask observation equal mass within site. Retain missing-prediction and missing-verification counts, rather than imply unchanged support. Resample the 16 calendar years with replacement; each sampled year carries all its storms, sites and issue offsets. Do not additionally resample storms independently. Use the same year draws across methods/contrasts. For random-mask designs resample R complete timeline IDs with replacement, shared across sites and issues, and average those R scores. This estimates uncertainty in the mask-mean effect, not the spread of one future outage. Enumerated schedules and network subsets are averaged exactly and are not resampled as random masks. Recompute the declared site-balanced estimand within each draw; if a required site has no paired support, retain a nonfinite draw and disclose it. Intervals require every draw finite. Few contributing years limit precision.

Primary paired effects are total EO increment, matched representation increment, donor restriction, and the direct interaction `(EO − C)_scarce − (EO − C)_rich` on paired cases. Report both conventional and augmented deterioration. K5/recent CRPS intervals are exploratory pointwise intervals for all episodes, high water and EO-supported opportunities; no family-controlled winner claim follows. Monthly, K10, archive caps, country, quality, equal-age and other endpoints are supporting diagnostics. Do not infer an interaction from two significance labels or choose a favorable model/severity after looking at scores.

The calendar is a full census of 481 declared rainfall episodes, not every possible high-water mechanism. Preserve all episodes and reuse the held independent p99 normalizer and reviewed flood register for evaluation-only strata: future p99 high water, non-high-water, undefined threshold, net rising/falling high water over the response window, and overlap with documented flood windows. Net change is not a claim of monotonic rise or an issue-time classification. p99 is not an agency flood stage. Keep whole-census-with-fallback, model-supported and common-weekly-EO-support estimates separate; usable support requires a query vector and eligible earlier donor vectors, not only an acquisition. Report native-unit site diagnostics and the original-versus-new local-method bridge. Earlier algorithm versions are not pooled as identical analyses.

Report effective observation age and the share of controlled-density cases retaining the same age as rich data; natural gaps can break that equality. Interpret network size with target retention, actual channel identities, Wurm redundancy, country and Maas availability. Maas is context, not a new forecast target. Finalized Q/rain and acquisition-safe EO are a retrospective nominal-availability reconstruction, not an as-issued replay; product-release times and checkpoint-pretraining overlap remain limitations. EO is antecedent catchment context, not an image of the developing flood. Inspect analogue dates, seasons and quality before assigning a physical mechanism to any improvement.

Save exact first-replication donor traces for all eligible cases, all replication-level score aggregates, source/config/code hashes, seed construction, issue/source clocks and restart-safe atomic job outputs. Full donor selections at any other replication can be reproduced from its saved mask and inputs; this bounds disk use without sampling the scientific census. Actual-case plots are labelled illustrations, not an outcome-selected operational policy.
