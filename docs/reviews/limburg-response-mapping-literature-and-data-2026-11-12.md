## Focused full-text methods review

The supplied paper was read in full, including SD1–SD4; its key results pages were visually checked. The other papers were reviewed for relevant methods, evaluation, labels, compute and limitations. This is a focused methodological review, not a systematic review or a claim that every reference cited by these papers was reviewed.

| Paper / access | What it contributes | What we should take, and avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Ratté-Fortin, Chokmani & Turcotte (2026)**, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jag.2026.105321), supplied full text + supplementary files | Event-separated transfer of separate S1/S2 U-Nets across 18 events; independent Quebec 2019 application; end-user motivation includes hydraulic-model calibration | Supports a receiving-agency, regional-transfer study. External Quebec assessment is **S2 only and one event**. Fold predictions do not make it many independent target floods. Its labels are sensor-visible **water/non-water**, not newly inundated land. It does not test receiving-region annotation allocation. Do not import its dB preprocessing into the linear-intensity Copernicus pipeline. |
| **Adhikari et al. (2025), ALFA**, [paper](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2025/1057.pdf), full text + supplement + pinned code | Human-guided flood-map correction, uncertainty/superpixel selection, explicit end-user workflow and measured annotation time | Strong direct response-mapping precedent. Uses **2 m aerial RGB + terrain**, two hurricane episodes/five regions and an objective of correcting the image itself. Its elevation-guided label propagation, canopy model and measured 20-minute annotation results do not transfer automatically to SAR or our simulation. |
| **Lee & Li (2024)**, [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.19043), publisher access blocked but full manuscript held | Flood-specific active learning compares **random, uncertainty and PCA/K-means diversity**; diagnoses ambiguity and class imbalance | Direct novelty overlap: the three query-policy categories are not new. Uses multispectral inputs, source-pool training and target-region validation/test splitting. Target validation consumption and spatial independence need explicit treatment in our design. Label-derived ambiguity indices are post-hoc diagnostics, not label-free query features. Its treatment of no-data as non-flood is not adopted. |
| **Shin, Xie & Albanie (2021), PixelPick**, [CVF paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021W/ILDAV/papers/Shin_All_You_Need_Are_a_Few_Pixels_Semantic_Segmentation_With_ICCVW_2021_paper.pdf), full text | Sparse-pixel supervision, loss only on acquired labels, spread/diversity under fixed annotation budgets | Supports partial labels within a larger image input and reporting annotation units carefully. Natural-image datasets and actual keyboard annotation experiments do not validate SAR annotation minutes. Its iterative training-from-scratch protocol is not our one-shot adaptation protocol. |
| **Lenczner et al. (2022), DIAL**, [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.01047), full text | Separates local annotation-as-input corrections from parameter updates that affect a wider image; compares query order and compute | Useful explanation of the correction-versus-transfer distinction. Mainly aerial building/land-cover segmentation, not a ready SAR flood implementation. Its simulated clicks can target known mistakes; our proposed queries must not inspect hidden error/flood labels. No extra DIAL architecture is introduced. |
| **Ryd & Nearing (2025), Fine Flood Forecasts**, [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.12559), full text + pinned code | Agency ownership and extra value of local data; global-to-local adaptation with multiple baselines | Supplies the organizational/comparison logic, **not** the segmentation architecture or a forecast claim. Basins are included in global pretraining; this is not proof of ungauged transfer. Per-basin tuning and local validation are real data/compute expenditures. No Caravan bulk download or new LSTM branch is needed. |
| **Selvam et al. (2024)**, [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09907), full text | Clay SAR segmentation and parameter-efficient adaptation | Supports adaptation as a legitimate EO task. Frozen-head adaptation cannot be presented as exhausting all backbone adaptation. Paper's hardware limits and custom multi-orbit setup do not justify claiming that LoRA or our method universally beats full tuning. No new LoRA branch required. |
| **Farajpoor & Narimani (2026)**, [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34904), full text | Comparable U-Net/random-initialization controls, label budgets, event exclusion, accuracy/compute trade-offs | Generic foundation-model-versus-U-Net label efficiency is already studied. Frozen and tuned models answer different questions. Avoid equating all target exclusions with a wholly nested unseen-event protocol when hyperparameters are shared. This is a preprint, not settled evidence. |
| **Wang et al. (2025), Copernicus-FM**, [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.11849), full text; author code already held | Native task architecture, metadata and normalization contract | Keep the supported paired-SAR implementation, metadata and normalizers. Cache frozen features only when crop/context/augmentation contracts are identical. A cached fixed encoder does not evaluate full fine-tuning. |
| **Chiriaco et al. (2026), GEOID-Flood**, [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02315), full text; held catalogue and benchmark shards | Paired/multimodal flood targets and geographic transfer; separate background/permanent/flood classes | Use exact delivered class, validity and source-reference provenance. New regional catalogue tiles remain candidates until timing, ground overlap and assessed-area support are reconciled. Manual validation does not mean independence from the same SAR image. |
| **Roberts et al. (2017)**, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/ecog.02881), open full text | Spatial/temporal dependence and matching blocked evaluation to the intended transfer task | Split unique ground support, not arbitrary overlapping tile IDs. Input-context overlap prevention is necessary but does not prove statistical independence. Choose/report separation for the intended within-region task; huge arbitrary blocks can inadvertently change it into a much harder extrapolation task. No universal buffer distance is imported. |
| **Wania et al. (2021)**, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs13112114), full text; official CEMS workflow pages held | Real mapping-service clocks: tasking, acquisition, image availability, map production/delivery | Grounds response relevance and the resource account. A better mapping experiment is not evidence of earlier warnings, fewer losses or actual service delivery speed. Use documented product delivery times where available; unknown agency receipt/annotation time stays unknown. |

### Supplied-paper cautions worth preserving

Its SD3 specifies recall-oriented Tversky tuning (S1 α=.35, β=.65; S2 α=.41, β=.52). These are not transferable local operational loss weights; false negatives and false positives should both be reported here. Pooled-pixel performance and equally weighted fold/event averages differ. Its discussion's convex-hull percentages (93.1%, 10.6%) differ from Figure 5/results (99.1%, 13.7%); this was verified visually. We do not rely on either set to justify regional representativeness. Failure to reject environmental dissimilarity is not proof of equivalence, and the covariance construction should not be copied without clarification. None of this prevents using the paper's event-separated evaluation and concrete receiving-agency motivation.

The new SAR acquisition had an initial **receipt serialization error**: Earth Engine mutates its download-parameter dictionary by adding an Image object. The only work needed before model fitting is **data preparation/design execution**, not another default dataset request: ground/context separation, source-fit lineage, reference rasterization and assessed support, label-unit/budget definitions, and an inexpensive source-only development/profile pass. This should determine attainable claims and compute, not become a requirement for favorable outcomes. We have not established future-flood transfer on the same local landscapes merely by downloading a different Rhine episode.

## Implications for the plan

Keep the RQ and comparator set. Explicitly position this as a regional empirical evaluation of **how much receiving-agency annotation helps outside the annotated places**, with full local-supervision costs and negative transfer visible. Random/uncertainty/diversity selection, active learning and fine-tuning are established methods. Their inclusion is a comparison design, not the novelty claim. The annotation/test separation, realistic receiving context, label accounting and observed limitations need to yield useful evidence; method count alone does not do so.

Retain one-shot selection unless an iterative experiment is deliberately approved later. Do not add ALFA's DEM/ForestNet, DIAL's annotation-channel model, HydroATLAS clustering, an optical branch, extra countries, or a new forecast experiment merely because these papers use them. The existing data support testing the scoped question; independent ground truth and measured operator annotation times remain limits to interpretation, not hidden promises or new mandatory acquisition gates.
