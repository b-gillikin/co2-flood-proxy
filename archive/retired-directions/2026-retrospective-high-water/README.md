# Retired Limburg directions, August–October 2026

This archive preserves completed or abandoned analytical directions that are
not components of the active basin-aware AI chapter.

- `docs/`: case-crossover protocol/results, rainfall-window follow-up,
  cross-tributary footprint, retrospective prediction, atmospheric increment,
  Wurm CO2/pressure and Maas diagnostics, early AI notes and superseded chapter
  outlines/reviews and the superseded student-action/IoT notes;
- `scripts/`: corresponding estimators, diagnostics and renderers;
- `tests/`: historical regression tests;
- `src/case_crossover.py` and `R/case_crossover_reference.R`: the validated
  Python/R case-crossover implementation.

Files were moved without rewriting internal historical links. They remain for
provenance and can be reconstructed in their original paths from Git history.
They are not maintained by the live test suite. Audited inputs and event-review
products remain live because the basin-aware experiment uses them.
